# End Hold Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** When the clip's last words carry an emphasis point, the rough cut keeps up to 1.5 s of footage after them, so the highlight text, graphic and sound on that point have time to show before the clip ends.

**Why:** project "1003 (1)" (2026-10-03). The speaker says "5 บาท" as the very last words; the rough cut ends 0.15 s after them (normal padding), so the price tag showed for about 0.5 s and no graphic was placed on it, though the direction asked for a big price tag with a sound.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest. Done means `npm test` (no new failures) and `npm run typecheck` (no new errors) from `prodeck2/`.

---

## Decisions

| # | Topic | Choice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | When | Only when the outline's **last beat is a speech beat** and an emphasis point on that beat has its **last word ending within 1.5 s of the end** of the beat's last kept word. A scene beat at the end already plays to its own end |
| 2 | How long | `END_HOLD_US = 1_500_000` after the last kept word, in place of the preset's padding |
| 3 | Bounds | Never past the next spoken word in the source (no extra words are heard) and never past the end of the video. So the hold can come out shorter than 1.5 s, or not at all when the footage ends there |
| 4 | Where | `compileCuts` gets an optional `endHoldUs`; `timeline.ts` decides with a two-pass compile: compile, check the end, compile again with the hold when it applies. `compile` is the one call site, so the preview, every work and the write all see the same cut |
| 5 | Old points | Points whose transcript fingerprint no longer matches the video are ignored, as everywhere else |
| 6 | No freeze frame | When the footage ends right after the words, nothing is added. A freeze frame is a possible follow-up |

## Task 1: core

- [x] `compileCuts({ …, endHoldUs?: number })`. Passed down to `speechCut` for the last beat only (the last beat in the list that is speech and is the final beat).
- [x] In `speechCut`, the final edge: when `endHoldUs` is given, `rawEndUs = min(words[lastIndex].endUs + endHoldUs, words[lastIndex + 1]?.startUs ?? ∞, clip.durationUs)`, and no snapping to a quiet spot (the hold is silence already, and snapping could pull it back toward the word). Without it, as today.
- [x] Tests: the last beat ends 1.5 s after its last word when the footage allows; stops at the next word; stops at the end of the video; other beats and `endHoldUs` absent are byte-identical to today; a scene beat at the end ignores it.

## Task 2: main

- [x] `endsOnPoint(stored, plan, clips)`: the last beat is speech; take its last kept word (the word that ends at or before the last piece's end); true when a speech point on that beat (`anchor.beatId`), whose video's fingerprint matches, has `words[anchor.to].endUs >= lastWordEnd - END_HOLD_US`.
- [x] `compile`: `plan = compileCuts(...)`; if `endsOnPoint` → `compileCuts({ ..., endHoldUs: END_HOLD_US })`.
- [x] Tests: a point on the last words lengthens the cut and the written timeline; a point earlier in the clip does not; no points does not; a stale fingerprint does not.

## Risks

- **Footage may end right after "5 บาท".** Then the cut cannot grow and this change does nothing for that project. The app can tell: the preview's length will not change.
- **The last 1.5 s may show the creator reaching for the phone.** The hold only applies after an emphasis point and stops at the next word; the user can trim it in CapCut.
- **Things already placed on the end** (the closing sound on the last beat's end, graphics that run to the end) move with the new end. Their anchors are words and beat edges, so they follow; graphics that end at the clip's end get longer, which is the point.
